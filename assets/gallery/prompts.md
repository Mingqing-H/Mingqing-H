# 图像手记 · 提示词

工具：内置 ImageGen。高山湖的提示词见 [alpine-twilight.prompt.txt](../alpine-twilight.prompt.txt)。


## fern

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: one panel of a curated personal-site photo gallery. Portrait close-up of a young fern unfurling after rain, one curved frond with pinpoint dew against layered deep moss greens. Soft light filtering through a Pacific Northwest forest, quiet fine art macro photography, real botanical structure, rich greens and finely resolved droplets. Frame the uncurling frond around the center with breathing room; approximately 4:5. No type, no logos, no frame, no collage.

## paper

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: a standalone visual in an art gallery mosaic. Sculptural folded paper arranged like a small canyon, softly ridged ivory sheets flowing into one burnt vermilion fold, striking cast shadows, tactile fine paper fibers. Fine-art studio still life, spare deliberate composition, warm bone-white ground, rust accent, realistic photographs of physical paper. Square or gently landscape crop, subject centered and crop-safe. No text, no logos, no frame, no collage.

## architecture

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: an editorial architecture photograph for a personal art gallery. A tall smooth sandstone arch set in quiet desert architecture, successive sunlit walls recede toward a small opening of cobalt sky, clean planes in peach, cream and rose. Realistic warm late-afternoon sunlight and distinct architectural shadows, restrained modern photography. Near-square composition with the arch opening centered and generous breathing room, no people, no text, no logos, no frame, no collage.

## jellyfish

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: an underwater gallery photograph. A luminous pale amber jellyfish drifting in deep indigo ocean, single delicate umbrella and naturally fine trailing tentacles, faint suspended particles, dark edges and soft concentrated light through the bell. Credible underwater macro photography with sophisticated restrained grading. Vertical 4:5, subject centered with safe space around the outer tentacles. No people, no type, no logos, no frame, no collage.

## city

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: a wide editorial photograph for an art gallery. An anonymous East Asian side street just after rain at blue hour, reflections of warm shop windows on wet paving, rows of simple dark façades and one distant blurred silhouette with an umbrella. Moody cobalt and orange practical light, analogue street photography without heavy grain, quiet atmosphere. Panoramic 2:1 composition, horizon and reflections in the central area, no readable signage, no type, no logos, no frame, no collage.
